RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on this 1st day of January 2026, at Bengaluru, Karnataka, by and between:

Mr. Rajesh V. Murthy, residing at Indiranagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter referred to as the "LANDLORD", which expression shall include his heirs, successors and assigns) of the FIRST PART;
AND
Ms. Ananya Sharma, working at Manyata Tech Park, Bengaluru (hereinafter referred to as the "TENANT", which expression shall include her legal representatives) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the absolute lawful owner of Residential Flat No. 402, Green Meadows Heights, 12th Main Road, HAL 2nd Stage, Indiranagar, Bengaluru - 560038 (hereinafter called the "Premises");

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM AND TENURE
The lease shall be for an initial period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st January 2026. The Tenant agrees to a mandatory 11-month lock-in period. If the Tenant vacates the Premises prior to the expiry of the lock-in period, the Landlord shall forfeit the entire Security Deposit without prejudice to claiming remaining months' rent.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND UTILITIES
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 45,000/- (Rupees Forty-Five Thousand only) in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. Any delay in payment beyond the 5th shall attract late interest of 24% per annum calculated on a daily basis forthwith.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DEDUCTIONS
The Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of INR 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs only). At the time of vacating, the Landlord reserves the sole and absolute discretion to determine painting charges, deep cleaning, and any wear and tear deductions.

4. UNRESTRICTED INSPECTION AND ENTRY
The Landlord or his authorized representatives shall be entitled to enter the Premises at all times without notice to inspect the condition of the flat or show the property to prospective buyers or tenants.

5. REPAIRS, ALTERATIONS AND STRUCTURAL DAMAGE
The Tenant shall maintain the Premises in immaculate condition. Any damage, whether minor electrical issues, major internal plumbing leaks, ceiling seepage, or wall cracks, shall be repaired solely at the Tenant's expense.

6. UNILATERAL TERMINATION
The Landlord reserves the unilateral right to terminate this lease forthwith upon verbal or written notice if the Tenant engages in any conduct deemed objectionable by the Landlord. Upon such notice, the Tenant shall vacate within 48 hours.

7. INDEMNITY AND GOVERNING LAW
The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord against all claims, losses, legal proceedings, and liabilities arising from the occupation of the Premises. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to courts in Bengaluru.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement on the day and year first written above.

LANDLORD: Rajesh V. Murthy
TENANT: Ananya Sharma
